About me

Hi there! I'm Brenden and welcome to my site! I'm a 3D artist who primarily focuses on creating fan art for the game Fortnite and some OC (original character) related stuff like artwork, stories and perhaps even games in the future! If you're curious about my work or have any questions, I've put together a small FAQ section that answers some of the most commonly asked questions I receive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you got a Support a Creator code?

I do! My creator code is "ItzBrenden".

Do you do commissions?

Due to personal reasons, such as time restraints and me not wanting to disappoint people with my work, I unfortunately don't do commissions of any kind. I do make some stuff for friends but this is usually because I choose to make it myself and not because they paid for it.

Can I repost your artwork?

No, I don't allow people to repost my stuff unless specific permission is granted or stated otherwise.

Can I make use your artwork for personal use?

Yes, of course! You're free to use my artwork for personal stuff like wallpapers, profile pictures, banners, etc. As long as there's no monetary gains, I don't mind. You can directly download some of my artwork from the portfolio page!

Can I make (NSFW) artwork of your characters?

For SFW art, of course! You generally don't have to even ask me, so if you have something cool in mind, go ahead! The only thing I ask is to keep the characters in mind for proper character representation. When it comes to NSFW artwork, however, I generally don't allow this unless you've gotten my personal permission to do so. Please contact me first through Twitter/X, Instagram or Discord if you are considering it.

Can I get a version of your art without the watermark?

No. I add the watermark for a reason after all. Unless it's a piece specifically made for someone else, you'll have to deal with that watermark.
