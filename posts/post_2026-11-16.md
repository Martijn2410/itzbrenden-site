# Regarding my absence.

As some of you may have noticed, I've been pretty absent from social media this year. Here’s a quick post explaining why, and what my plans are going forward.

Before I get into that, though, I just wanna say how nice it’s been to actually live life without worrying about some stupid internet point system lmao.

Anyway, my absence comes down to two simple things:

First, **college**. This was my final year, and I just wanted to lock in and focus on graduating lol. In case you're wondering, I officially graduated this summer with a bachelor's in Information & Communication Technology!

Second, **a huge lack of motivation**. I've been playing Fortnite since 2018 and creating art for it since 2019. It's 2026 now, and honestly, the spark has just run out. The game isn't really what I originally fell in love with. Whether that's for better or worse isn't what I'm here to debate, though. Combine that with all the bullshit Epic Games has been pulling in 2026 alone, and it's just really hard to stay motivated.

So, the big question: **Am I quitting? Is this the end of my art journey?**

My honest answer is simply, "I don't know yet." I still love working in Blender and making cool pieces for you guys, so I'm definitely not closing that door completely. At the same time, I want to explore my other passions. I'm a software developer with a background in game dev, so I'd love to head in that direction moving forward. Maybe I'll start making art for other games, or maybe Fortnite does something crazy with Chapter 8 that hooks me right back in. Who knows.

For now, though, I'm going to be focusing on those other projects. I'll still be around, just expect my art posts to massively slow down, or pause entirely, while I figure out what's next.

Thank you to those who stayed with me throughout the years, and hopefully you'll still be around for my other projects as well!
