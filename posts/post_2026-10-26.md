# A simple blogging feature!

Today I introduced a simple blogging feature to my website!

The purpose of this blogging feature is to document changes I have made to my website and perhaps even other projects, but also to share some important news if I have anything to share.

This blog is made possible by basic HTML, CSS and JS, while the blog posts themselves are written in Markdown.

Currently, I can write my blog posts in markdown. Add an entry to a JSON file so my post index knows what posts exist. And with a bit of JS and Marked.js, posts can get indexed, displayed and rendered in live environments.
